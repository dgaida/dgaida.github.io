An adaptive Retrieval‑Augmented Generation system called Living Brain has been developed to consolidate the scattered, frequently outdated insurance knowledge used by small‑business sales teams, automatically indexing documents, answering German queries with source citations, and detecting knowledge gaps. A usage‑driven feedback loop continuously re‑ranks results based on confidence, frequency and recency, while all processing runs locally to remain GDPR‑compliant.
